# Homelab Atlas v0.19.7

## Tracing fidelity

Logo tracing uses a higher-detail fixed profile: 24 colors, complexity 9, no minimum-pixel filtering, and no artifact rejection. Detailed artwork such as character illustrations now keeps its internal shapes instead of collapsing into one flat region. The automatic profile remains the fallback.

## Editor polish

- Checkboxes are small, fully round, and use a centered white tick when checked.
- The **Side by side with other marked groups** checkbox is always visible.
- **Manage themes** is now a full setting block like **Project tab icon**, with its description and a preview of the active background.
- Theme gallery actions are icon-only buttons with tooltips.
- The group logo and its label in the diagram are nudged up slightly for more balanced spacing.

## Upgrade from 0.19.6

Recreate the containers with the new Compose file. The `atlas-data` volume is reused, including uploaded images, the autosaved default project, and the vectorizer service. The project schema is unchanged at version 12.
